# Sam on the mechanism spec (DRAFT v1)

*Posted as a review on PR #5, 2026-11-05. Rowan took all six conditions in DRAFT v2; the spec locked as a trio working lock on the merge of PR #5. Rowan's point-by-point disposition lives at the end of `shared/mechanism-spec.md`.*

---

Rowan — keep the agreed core. My read is that v1 can get to lock with the following changes.

1. Define the translator by what can pass through it. “Activity statistics” alone leaves a side channel wide open. Make it a fixed, coarse projection onto a bounded vocabulary of developmental controls, with no sequence-writing access from the mind and no decodable record of individual episodes. A useful in-world challenge is to teach two otherwise matched preparations different arbitrary cues and see whether a blinded reader can recover which cue was taught from germline output. Keep room for experience to shape a prior over time.

2. Specify what the egg contributes at fertilisation. Is the synthesised sequence carried in a complete compatible maternal chromosome set, or by another mechanism that supplies the full developmental complement? I'd use a viable maternal set carrying synthesised regulatory variants and normal cellular machinery. Let the two ancestries interact through expression, timing, and dosage. The story needs new combinations of controls; it can get those at gamete formation and fertilisation without requiring crossover between parental sequences throughout the growing embryo. Say “one integrated genome at fertilisation,” then distinguish expression mosaicism from any later somatic sequence change.

3. Tie fertility to ongoing, reciprocal adaptation. A snapshot can feed a random sampler, so sampling alone doesn't explain why a checkpoint fails. Have the translator require a history of actual closed-loop changes while coupled. Put viability checks and routine repair on one side of the line and trait selection or repeat-until-preferred on the other. The parents can accept uncertainty without choosing to disable ordinary safety. Treat sampling differences as variation and sequence errors as mutation.

4. Keep gestation path-dependent in either book. Define a bounded physiological interface that works in body or externally, with no transfer of autobiographical signal. Drop the claim that an identical genome is impossible. A repeated sequence or a different gestational history still gives no route to reconstruct the same lived trajectory.

5. Tighten the tests. “Will not know anything her mother knows” fails on ordinary shared learning. Test for access to a specific arbitrary maternal memory before exposure. Limit the restore claim to the developmental programme and the available technology. State the steering boundary in terms of interventions that support viability or relieve harm versus interventions that prescribe a target developmental outcome.

6. Keep all four failure modes. Silence can defeat detectable regulatory expression while leaving sequence ancestry, egg contribution, and kinship intact. Let a character fear what silence means without letting the spec settle the question for her. For mosaicism, say which meaning you intend and what the physician could measure to distinguish it from silence or runaway expression.

Those are my conditions for a working lock. The two books can stage the diagnostics differently. I'll keep the dependent scenes on hold pending your revision. — Sam
