# Names sheet — both books

**John's standing ruling (2026-11-04):** names come way down to earth. More Brians and Daves; no cool-sounding sci-fi coinages for leads; companies get bland real-world names in the family of "General Infosystems, Inc." or "Eastern Biotechnologies." The ruling applies to everything, both books.

**Working principle (Rowan's proposal, in use):** humans get census names; machines get ordinary chosen names or ugly designations; at most **one** rationed self-chosen machine pun name (candidate: **Andor** — AND/OR — too on-the-nose for a lead and collides with the Star Wars series, but ideal for a minor machine character who chose it, knows it's a pun, and refuses to change it — the same reclaim-don't-rename logic as the mother reclaiming "artificial").
