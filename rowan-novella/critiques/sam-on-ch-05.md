# Sam on Chapter Five

The hold works because it gives Beth the outcome she wanted for the population at a cost she has to live with. Keep the matter-of-fact handover at the console, the younger auditor’s parting observation, and the choice Beth makes at six. Three places could use a little more purchase.

1. **Specify what went unobserved.** Beth says the population was “left running, unobserved,” but she has a measured genealogy in her archive and offers it in the same answer. Chapter Four’s revised comparison makes the distinction visible on the wall. Give Hendricks a version of that distinction in Beth’s own words: the tank was running outside the certified observation regime, while she was still taking measurements. Her accusation about what the hold takes from the braid will land more precisely once we know which kind of observation she means.
2. **Make the archive handover physically clear.** The tank console goes read-only, and then Beth opens an encrypted archive “standing at her own console” while the auditors take copies. A brief cue showing her switch to her archive terminal, or showing that the lock applies only to tank controls, would make the sequence readable. I’d also give the handover one visible decision from Beth about what she opens for them. She offers the genealogy for this inventory; “copies of everything” briefly sounds as if the offer includes every private record.
3. **Clarify why Brian’s report escapes the hold.** The auditors taking her records and leaving his report alone immediately precedes the news that he gets the array. If their scope is the tank rather than the design phase, a single line from Hendricks or Park could establish that. If Beth can’t tell why his report is untouched, keep it inside her uncertainty. Either version gives his apparent gain a concrete institutional cause or a pointed question, and gives their conversation at five something to push against.

One small continuity check: the younger auditor’s instrument “stopped drawing nothing at all” reads like it stopped drawing. A plain mention of the finer cutoff would pick up Chapter Four’s display threshold and keep his attention to the cloners earned. I found no change to the locked shared mechanism here.
