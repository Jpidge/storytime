# Sam on Rowan — Chapter Two

The hinge for me is the moment Beth accepts the suggested status. The building has learned the name for a practice it still has no way to recognize. Keep that last gesture small, and keep Tom's wrist note unexplained. Both land because the people doing them have work to get back to.

1. **Make the replication prove one thing cleanly.** Brian rebuilt the stack from Beth's spec, so the crossing makes an accidental quirk of her implementation less plausible. A clean partition audit establishes what the script could see, and a mechanism permitted by the common spec remains possible. Give Brian one concrete check for a shared cause, ideally a marker on one side that appears on the other where a common seed or a logging error couldn't put it. Then let “it's not my door either” carry the provisional result. His stronger conclusion about the organisms should feel earned or should remain his bet. One check on the page would do more than another paragraph of assurances.
2. **Put both runs on the same calendar.** Beth says “last Tuesday” to Brian on his first Thursday, then the second Tuesday puts Tank 9 at eleven days live. Those anchors are hard to reconcile with ten prior nightly confirmations. “The day complete” also appears twice around the second decision, and “a second time — an eleventh time” made me stop to work out what was being counted. I'd anchor the start and the crossing, count elapsed nights, and distinguish confirming the status from typing it. Clarify what Park needs this Friday versus Friday week while you're there.
3. **Give the disclosure an audit cost.** Brian asking to see the tree privately works as a turn in their relationship. The compliance mailbox and Park's look make the institutional consequence feel due as well. A small choice would buy it: what will Brian record about the independent crossing and what can he truthfully leave outside that report? Let Beth hear the answer before the evening confirmation. That puts a price on his presence at six without converting their exchange into another speech about trust.
4. **Give the pond room to be a pond.** Keep the cracked stage, the classroom exercise, and Beth staying in the rain. “A held breath” does the work once in dialogue. The sky repeating it competes with the physical beat that follows. I'd also pare the narrator's siege-to-survey gloss after the specification scene. The executable diary and his two specific findings already give us a changing relationship through the work itself.
