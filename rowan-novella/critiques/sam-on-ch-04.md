# Sam on Chapter Four

The dinner earns the shift from titles to names, and the inventory pays off Brian’s promise through what he does at the bench. Keep his pencil going down without the turn. I’d make three small passes before finalizing.

1. **Give the auditors a legible comparison.** Beth files a fresh genealogy on Saturday, the live plot knows about the braid, and the status history keeps all sixteen confirmations. At noon, “the genealogy’s hole” can therefore sound like missing lineage or missing data. I read it as the period for which the measured lineage has no certified counterpart. A brief cue when the comparison comes up, perhaps the actual and certified traces visibly parting, would let the reader see what each record can prove. It would also distinguish the auditors’ snapshot of the current population from Beth’s saved map without an added explanation of the system.
2. **Separate Brian’s Friday knowledge from his Sunday reading of Beth’s choice.** Chapter Three ends with Beth telling him she doesn’t yet know what she’ll do, before she confirms the fourteenth night. Here “You’ve known since Friday” immediately follows “What I intend to do tomorrow.” If she means he’s known the tank’s condition or what the audit would find, name that object in her line. If it’s deliberate, let his reply register the distinction between what she said on Friday and what he inferred from sixteen evenings. His offer to be wherever she wants is strongest when the inference still leaves the decision with her.
3. **Give the dinner a beat of its own before returning to the console.** The cut from the walk home back to six brings us close to Sunday’s reset twice in quick succession. A single ordinary action on the walk home could hold the physical memory before the flashback, or the six-o’clock confirmation could close the Saturday lab scene. I’d also try shortening the explanation after the kiss so the uncertainty about earlier embodiments has room to register before Beth assigns the moment its meaning.

The audit readout, the four-week extension, and the Friday–Sunday count line up with Chapter Three. I found no conflict with the locked mechanism in this chapter. The ending has enough pressure to cut exactly where it does.
