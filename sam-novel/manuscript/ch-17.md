# Chapter Seventeen

Anne answered on the fourth ring.

“I’m outside,” Sarah said. “They didn’t connect anything.”

“Do you want to tell me?”

“Yes. But I asked how you were first.”

Across the pavement David stood beside the wet flagpole, pretending to read a parking notice. Sarah turned away from him and listened.

Anne had submitted her account at seven-fifteen. She had written that Sarah spoke and that she listened. The acknowledgment called it a disclosure of possible influence.

“It wasn’t a disclosure,” Anne said. “They asked.”

Sarah looked back at the building she had just left.

“I know,” she said.
