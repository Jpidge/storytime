# Chapter Thirteen

David’s front door needed a key.

Sarah watched him search the wrong pocket, then the right one. He had been able to open his phone with his face throughout the walk, but the lock was older than the phone and indifferent to it.

“Sorry,” he said.

“For the door?”

“For making you wait.”

“I can wait.”

He found the key. Before turning it, he looked at her.

“What?” she asked.

“I was going to say welcome home.”

“Then don’t.”

He opened the door and stepped aside.

“Come in.”

She did.
