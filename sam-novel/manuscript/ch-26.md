# Chapter Twenty-Six

By the time they got back to the road, the van was running. The installer had set his tablet on the passenger seat, face down. He lowered his window when Sarah stopped beside it.

“Did it keep the note?”

He turned the tablet over. She could see the little picture of a cloud in the corner. There was a line through it.

“It’ll go when I get up the hill,” he said.

She stood back. He pulled out slowly, his front wheel going into the rut David had gone around. When the van straightened she could see the patch of road behind it where the wheel had brought up water.

Anne was in the back seat with her coat undone. She asked Sarah if they were going home.

“In a minute.”

Sarah called Helen again. She gave her the job number this time. Helen read it back in groups of three, with a pause between each group. Sarah followed with her finger on the receipt.

“Have you found it?” she asked.

“I’ve got a job with that number,” Helen said. “What did he say he was saving it as?”

“Unfinished.”

“Then wait with me a second. Mine says it hasn’t started.”
