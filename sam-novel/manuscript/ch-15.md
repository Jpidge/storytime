# Chapter Fifteen

At nine-twenty-three Ellen answered the question Sarah had forwarded.

*No. Do not use that definition pending clarification.*

Sarah read it standing beside the bed. David was asleep on his side, one hand open on the sheet. She had thought an answer would let her put the phone down. Instead she wanted to know who else had received it.

Midwest was copied.

She saved the message without replying.

In the kitchen the pan still soaked in cold water. She pressed a thumb against the loosened rice and watched it separate from the metal.

Her phone sounded again.
