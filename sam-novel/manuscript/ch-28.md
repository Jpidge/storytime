# Chapter Twenty-Eight

The next day Bill called while Sarah was washing out a cup. He had asked Helen to put his number on the request, but the reply had gone to the gatehouse address. He said he was looking at it on the office computer.

“Is it the sheet?” Sarah asked.

“It’s a link to the sheet.”

She set the cup on the draining board. “What happens when you open it?”

“It wants me to say I’m the person who signed.”

In the kitchen the refrigerator stopped. For a moment she could hear the room Bill was in. Something tapped against the glass at his end, and he put a hand over the receiver. When he came back he said Betty was waiting for him to look at the grass with her.

“Can you leave that page open?” Sarah asked.

“I can leave it open.”

“Then go with her. Call me when you get back.”

Bill said all right, but stayed on the line. Sarah heard him take something out of a drawer.

“I’m putting the time in the book,” he said. “In case the page goes away.”
