# Chapter Twenty-Nine

The printer pulled the second page in at an angle. Bill took his hand off the phone and caught the paper before it dropped behind the desk.

“Is it all there?” Sarah asked.

He flattened it against the book. “I can see what I said.”

“Does it have the job number on it?”

There was a pause while he looked. He read the number from the first page and then from the second. Sarah put a tick beside the number on her receipt. She heard him take the pages apart again.

“Who do I give it to if they send somebody else?” he asked.

Through the kitchen window Sarah could see Anne at the washing line. She had taken the pegs out of the bag and was putting them back one at a time. The phone was warm against Sarah’s face.

“Keep that copy,” she said. “Can you print another?”

Bill said he could. Then Betty asked something from the other side of the office. Bill moved the receiver away from his mouth.

“She wants to know which name’s on the top,” he said.
