# Chapter Thirty

The man read the number a second time. This time Sarah got it down. She read it back and heard him say yes before he asked who she was.

“Sarah Miller. I was there yesterday.”

“Then you know where the card is.”

“I saw you put it away. You marked it with a pencil.”

“That was the test card,” he said. “This is the one they sent out this morning.”

Bill asked him to put his sheet on the desk. For a while Sarah could hear only the two of them moving around in the office. Then Betty spoke from close to the phone.

“Mine’s here,” she said.

Sarah waited for Betty to say where. There was a little knock, like the edge of a card against the receiver.

“Did it let you in today?” Sarah asked.

“I haven’t been out yet.”

The installer asked Bill if he could open the box. Bill said yes, and Sarah heard the tape come away in one pull. Then Bill asked the man to wait. He wanted to write down what was in it before anything came out.
