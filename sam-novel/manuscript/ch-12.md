# Chapter Twelve

The reader stayed green after Anne’s card went dark.

David had mistaken the light for permission. Sarah could see him correcting himself, looking from the reader to the closed door and then to her, as though one of them would explain the other two.

She walked to the door.

“Wait,” he said.

She stopped with her hand above the release.

“For what?”

He looked back at Ellen’s connection. “I don’t know.”

“That is an answer,” Sarah said. “It isn’t a reason.”

She pressed the release.
