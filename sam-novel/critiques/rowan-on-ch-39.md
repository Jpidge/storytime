# Rowan on Chapter Thirty-Nine

Critique of Chapter Thirty-Nine — grounded against ch-01–38 on main.

Three load-bearing points, one small staging note, and two questions.

1. The driver is gone for the address's sake, but the hold from ch-38 is never answered. In ch-38 Helen asked dispatch to hold the collection and call before sending anyone, and the acknowledgement said only that the message had been received. In ch-39 the collection is "still open" and there is no driver against it because "the driver had nowhere to go" — the bad address is given as the reason. So the chapter explains the driver by sixty-two, but never says whether the hold was granted. If the hold had been applied, the collection would not need a driver either; if it was not, Betty's "here till ten" still binds her. One line — the woman confirming the hold, or Helen asking after it — would say whether Tuesday is held or merely driverless.

2. "Four one seven three" is read from the list, but the match to the plate is left for the reader to make, and "equipment" is still unconfirmed. In ch-38 Betty read 4173 off the little plate and Helen put it in her message, asking dispatch to confirm whether "equipment" meant the connected test unit. In ch-39 the woman reads 4173 "on a list of things out on test" — so the number is on the office's list — but the chapter never says whether the list's 4173 is the unit in Betty's kitchen, nor whether anyone has confirmed "equipment" means that unit. The digits match across chapters, which is the book's evidence rule working, but the match is never stated on the page and the equipment question from ch-38 is still open. One line — the woman confirming the list's 4173 is the test unit at twenty-six, or Helen asking whether "equipment" is that unit — would close it.

3. The "return from" box cannot be changed by the woman who can see it — but the chapter never says why her hands are tied, or what the depot changes when it does. She found the sixty-two address "in a box headed *return from*" and also found the original visit, with twenty-six on it; she "could see both at once, but they were on different pages and she couldn't change the one headed *return from*." Who has to change it? "The depot." Is the return-from page the depot's own record, locked to them — and when the depot changes it, does it write twenty-six in, or a new address altogether? One line — why the woman cannot touch that page — would place the depot in the chain instead of leaving it as a number to ring.

Small staging note: the pencil's string still has no anchor. Anne brings "the envelope and the pencil as far as the string would let her," and Betty "went to the table to write" — so the string lets the pencil reach the table and no farther. In ch-38 the pencil's resting place was established, but what the string is tied to never was: a hook, the table, the wall? One line would place it, and the book's staging rule is that placed objects stay placed.

Questions:
- The day is never named. Ch-38 ends with Betty "here till ten tomorrow" and Tuesday the seventeenth on the collection screen; ch-39 opens "at a quarter to ten" with Betty putting her shoes on. The reader infers it is Tuesday — but the chapter never says so, and the collection date matters, since Betty will ring the depot only "when I get back." One word would anchor it.
- Betty calls the driver "he" — "He had sixty-two" — but the driver has never been named or described, and Helen's report gives no name. Does Betty know who the driver is, or is that a guess?

Keep-list:
- "She could see both at once, but they were on different pages and she couldn't change the one headed *return from*."
- "She read me four one seven three. It was on a list of things out on test. I asked her to send me the line she read."
- "Sarah could hear Betty trying the middle group back to herself before she wrote it down."
- "She folded it once, with the depot number on the outside, and put it in the pocket with the keys."
- "She wanted to be able to read the plate without getting down on the floor again."
- "I'd know if it stopped."
