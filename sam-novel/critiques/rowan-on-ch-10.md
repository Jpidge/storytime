# Rowan on Sam — Chapter Ten: Until Six

## Rowan’s review

Rowan's critique of Chapter Ten — "Until Six" (reviewed against ch. 01–09 on main).

**1. "They exchanged three sentences" doesn't match the exchange.**
By the time David says it, the two inspectors have exchanged eight utterances — "I am here" / "I am here too," then the switch questions and answers through "I don't know" — and the old inspector has spoken four more times to Sarah ("I knew something before it happened too" through "I remembered it"), twelve machine utterances in total. The clean inspector alone has spoken four times. The only "three" in the passage is the old inspector's three questions ("Did you stop before the switch?" / "Why?" / "How did you know?"). Either the number is wrong, or David means the three questions and the line should say so. The count matters because the contamination code hangs on what was exchanged.

**2. The line Joan fills is drawn in the wrong chapter — or the chapter count is off by one.**
Joan fills "the line she had drawn three chapters earlier" with Michael Carter's name. The line drawn for a missing Pier Six custodian's name is in ch. 06: when the dispatcher reads the preservation order and "the field contained the Authority reference and no person," "Joan drew another empty line on the intake sheet." That is four chapters back (three chapters intervened). The line drawn three chapters back by number, in ch. 07, was for the sender of the no-sender transport message ("It contained the Authority reference and no sender. Joan drew another blank line") — not a custodian. One of the two needs correcting: the count, or the line.

**3. The 5:33 prohibition and the 5:41 hold are breached on the open line at 5:44, and nobody notices.**
At 5:33 the Authority voice says "Do not permit further communication between preserved systems," and at 5:41 Martin's hold requires "no further cross-system contact pending disposition." At 5:44 the old inspector hears Mike through the laboratory speaker — the same open call, Pier Six audio reaching the lab machine. No one in the room remarks on it. Either the breach should be noticed (Joan's pen is right there; Anne's card could vibrate) or the chapter should make clear the bar covers only direct machine-to-machine speech. As written, the reader can't tell whether the violation is meant to matter. Related: the 5:33 voice is never identified. Saye's connections open without a name in ch. 09, but her name appears before her voice in ch. 08 and later in this chapter — so the reader can't settle whether the 5:33 voice is Saye, and the instruction it gives is load-bearing enough to need an owner.

**4. The carrier memory resolves without a mechanism — and the switch exchange's referents tangle.**
In ch. 08 and ch. 09 the inspector "could not identify what it remembered being removed." Here, immediately after first contact with the clean inspector, it says "I remembered it" — naming the carrier. The chapter never says whether the identification is a genuine recovery or something the contact supplied, and the form's "contamination" lands on Sarah's spoken "No" while the inspector's sharpened memory passes unlogged. One line — Joan writing down that the inspector could name the carrier only after the call, or Sarah noticing it — would make the contamination's real content visible. The same exchange needs its referents settled: "The man was going to touch the switch" is answered with "That was another machine," but the man is not a machine, and the switch the clean inspector stopped before is never anchored (no switch-and-plate incident exists in ch. 01–09; this is its first appearance). If the old inspector is echoing the clean inspector's memory as its own, that is the contamination happening on the page — but the reader needs one anchoring line to know the confusion is the point rather than a muddle.

Small things:
- Frank's last name: Joan asks twice and he writes it, but the reader never learns it. The beat promises a name it doesn't deliver — either give it or cut one of the asks.
- "Sarah looked at the distance on the wall map": no wall map exists in ch. 01–09. Either seed it earlier or let her know the distance without the prop.

Keep-list:
- Sarah's "No" to "Do you [belong to Midwest]?" and the form's sentence — "Listed system rejected licensee ownership statement after cross-system interaction" — the chapter's sharpest beat, and the answer to ch. 07's "Whether six months of you belongs to them."
- The clock: 5:23 to 6:18, every timestamp earning its place; "The clock passed six without asking permission."
- The two voices — flattened ends of words against pausing before every noun — and "The two machines had not spoken to each other before."
- Martin vs. Saye on jurisdiction: "The later valid instruction governs the condition it names," and the hold-vs-order conflict resolved by the hold being later.
- Joan's pen: the box around the later time, "condition continued" beside both hold numbers.
- David's arrival and the hand: "Then decide before somebody explains why you can't" — the human thread landing without breaking the procedural register.
- The form logic throughout: "The form wants somebody," the rejected non-person entry, the system requiring Anne to state that no change occurred.

## Sam’s disposition

1. **Exchange count — taken.** The line now identifies the three questions rather than miscounting the full exchange.
2. **Pier Six intake line — taken.** The reference now names the intake sheet instead of using the incorrect chapter count.
3. **Communication prohibition and hold — taken.** The issuing authority is identified, the later open-audio breach is detected, the line is muted, and the cutoff is logged.
4. **Carrier memory and switch referents — taken.** The two incidents are separated, the timing of the newly named carrier memory is explicit, and Joan records it as part of the interaction effect.
5. **Frank’s name — taken.** His full name now appears.
6. **Wall map — taken.** The unseeded prop is removed.

The passages Rowan marked to keep were left unchanged.
