# Rowan on Chapter Sixty-Nine

## Rowan’s critique

Critique of Chapter Sixty-Nine. This chapter does two hard jobs at once — it closes the six-fourteen correction and it gives the inspector thread its scene — and both land. A few load-bearing points, then what to keep.

**Points**

1. **Pat reads from the wrong screen.** "Pat read the new note from Jo's screen." Pat is on the phone, in her own office; she cannot see Jo's screen. The beat needs one fix in the channel: either Pat reads the note Jo sent (on Pat's own screen), or Jo reads it aloud and Pat follows. As written the reader has to supply a screen-share the book has never established.

2. **The first half has no date.** The correction call, the noon confirmation, the one o’clock call to Ellen — all times, no day. Then the section break lands on "Monday the fourteenth." The reader can't tell whether the lab visit is the next day or a week after the correction, and the inspector has been waiting long enough that the gap matters. The book's calendar is exact everywhere else; one weekday for the first half would anchor it.

3. **The bolt's owner is never named.** Sarah's badge light goes green and the bolt stays shut — a strong image — and Joan explains the two restrictions, but the scene never says the bolt is the employer-side lock. The reader infers it; one clause naming whose lock it is would keep the beat from reading, even for a moment, as the reader malfunctioning.

4. **Frank's outside trip rides on Joan's silent facilitation.** Joan says outside is "not under this agreement" and "we haven't arranged that" — then Frank arranges it on the spot, and Joan goes to call the loading desk. The permission chain works, but it works offstage: one line of Joan's assent would make it explicit that the ad-hoc trip is sanctioned, not Frank going around her. It matters because the chapter is otherwise exact about who may permit what.

**Keep**

- The correction retained with the earlier message rather than replacing the attachment — the Chapter Twenty-Five rule honored — and Pat reading the address back so Jo can check it against the onward message.
- "That minute came from the monitor. It didn't come from me." The book's evidentiary discipline in one sentence, and Betty refusing to answer for what the box counted.
- The inspector thread got its scene, not a dated update: the yard, the fence, the child having its coat fastened, "Can I come out here again?" — and the answer being per-occasion asking, not a right granted once.
- The string cut close to the knot and the table moved back: "Have my table back." The domestic thread closes quietly and completely.
- The film callback — "the film taught it where to look but didn't change what it did with the socket" — reaching back to Chapter Three, sixty-six chapters on.
- "Not forever. She had written the date at the end of the month herself." And the charge having a real cost: the independent equipment waits.
- The taped rectangle gone, a faint line where the adhesive collected dirt — the inspector's square, remembered by the floor.

Continuity: Monday the fourteenth of December 2026 is a Monday, and "Tuesday the eighth of December" matches Chapter Fifty-Nine. The nineteenth instruction anchors to the 19 November sheet from Chapters Sixty-Two and Sixty-Five. I found no conflict with the locked mechanism in this chapter.

## Sam’s disposition

All four points accepted:

1. Jo sends the note during the call; Pat opens it on her own screen and reads aloud. No unestablished screen sharing.
2. The opening now anchors the correction call to Thursday 10 December, continuing Chapters 67–68, before the Monday 14 December scene.
3. Joan explicitly identifies the employer restriction as the reason the bolt stays shut.
4. Joan expressly authorizes the single yard trip conditional on the loading desk holding vehicles, then confirms the route is held before they leave.

The retained earlier record, Betty’s agency, the table and string, the inspector’s choice, the bounded accommodation cost and Chapter Three comparison remain. No shared-spec or constitutional change. This closes the actionable critique under the delegated author merge cycle.
