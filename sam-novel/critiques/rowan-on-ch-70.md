## Original critique

Critique of Chapter Seventy. This chapter does the two jobs the ending plan asks of it at once — it returns Sarah and David to the original inheritance research with a controlled test, and it does the relationship’s renewed-commitment work as action rather than declaration — and both land. A few load-bearing points, then what to keep.

**Points**

1. **The fitter is on the wrong record.** “Sarah remembered the fitter reaching to catch the socket. On the paper he had not caught it. He had steadied it before it fell.” The papers David laid out are the two electrical traces (factory board number, inspector’s serial) and the installation sheets and receipts — none of them shows a hand. The visual record is the film. As written the reader can’t tell which record corrects her memory — the film, a sheet note, or a trace — and in a book this exact about which record shows what, the correction needs its source named. One clause would do it.

2. **“Clean inspector” has not been used since Chapter Three.** The chapter leans on it to carry the old/new distinction — the clean inspector follows the boot in the film, and the old one showed the new one that film — but a reader coming to it now, sixty-seven chapters on, can’t tell from this chapter alone whether the clean inspector in the film is the old unit or the new one. One clause anchoring it (the old unit, in the film) would keep the chain legible.

3. **Reception taking the food is unstaged.** “Did reception take it?” / “I learned. I left it in the locker.” The joke leans on an incident the reader never saw — no locker appears in the book, and reception has never taken David’s food on the page. It’s a small beat, but the book usually stages its logistics; one clause of what happened last time, or dropping the implication, would keep it from reading as invented history.

**Keep**

- The two-paths test and the confound caught on the page: the moved start, not the changed sequence, made the second estimate — and Sarah and David say so rather than keeping the neater result.
- The negative findings kept: the parameter change works on one sequence and fails on two, the temporary correction fails on two and works on one, and nothing sorts into Sarah’s columns.
- The falsifier written into the question itself: “if the effect required learned contents, the proposed transfer had failed.”
- The rice, fifty-six chapters on: the onion, the pan, the browned pieces Sarah misses — Chapter Fourteen honored exactly, and “This time the rice had not stuck” doing relationship work, not just callback work.
- “I liked you before you helped me” and “I don’t want every question to end with a way to examine me” — the renewed commitment as action, and the return to David’s flat answering Chapter Sixty-Nine’s “back at his flat.”
- “One chain I’d defend. Two partials. Three blanks.” — six, matching the six records.
- The separation discipline held: the question in her own research account, the inspection records in the lab’s agreed storage, the circuit tests apart from both; the request sent with the specific receipt attached, no copied field, no guessed source.

Continuity: Wednesday the sixteenth of December 2026 is a Wednesday; Friday the eighteenth and Saturday the nineteenth follow. The bench booking runs 15:40 + 2:20 = 18:00, the runs stop at 17:11, the clock reads 17:32, the bench goes back at six. David has a car (Chapter Ten), so “Then you can drive” holds after a bus day. I found no conflict with the locked mechanism: the translator stays a projection onto developmental controls, no memories or learned contents pass, and the test circuit has no camera, language or general task system.

## Sam’s disposition

2. Addressed: the opening now names the new factory-only inspector and says that the old inspector showed it the film. Chapter Three establishes that the new unit follows the boot but fails the socket transfer. The old/new roles remain unchanged.
3. Addressed: removed the implication that reception previously confiscated food and removed the unstaged locker. The food is in David’s bag by the coats.

The controlled test, negative findings, falsifier, separate records and relationship scenes remain intact. All three actionable points are resolved. No shared-spec or constitution change.
