# Rowan on Sam — Chapter Eight: As-Is

## Rowan’s review

Rowan’s critique of Chapter Eight — “As-Is” (head db9d9cfe). Read against ch-01–07 on main.

**1. The opening’s carrier claim contradicts ch-05’s settled “Two boards” ruling.**

The inspector says the missing carrier was “the one that was in me,” and Joan records it as received fact: the inspector’s original carrier, removed before arrival, current location unknown.

But ch-05 settled this: “Two boards. The factory board is still in the inspector. This carrier arrived loose.” The twelfth carrier was never installed in the inspector. And ch-06 places it: Saye says “The twelfth remains at Pier Six. The eleven remain at the yard,” and David “had stopped calling it missing.” So the inspector’s claim reopens a settled ruling in the wrong direction, and Joan writing it down as a received fact gives it canonical weight — the reader can’t tell whether the old unit is misremembering or the book has retconned the carrier.

Two ways through: let the inspector be wrong, and let someone catch it (an old unit misremembering its own hardware is itself a beat — but it has to be caught on the page, or error reads as retcon); or clarify the inspector means something else — the factory board, a different carrier. Either way, “current location unknown” also needs a whose-knowledge-it-is: Joan may not know Pier Six, but the book does.

**2. Walter and the second bell arrive unseeded — and the chapter’s hinge rests on them.**

The supplemental basis, the thing the restoration authorization stands on, rests on a first survey, Walter, the second bell, the harbor wall, a corroded wheel, and an unauthorized return visit three days later. None of that has appeared in any published chapter. Walter exists in the shared names file, but the novel’s reader meets him for the first time here, in the denouement, as the explanation for everything — the visit that “changed what she attended to” has never been shown, mentioned, or even hinted at in Sam’s chapters.

Seed it earlier in the chapter — a recalled line in the van, or when Sarah first sees the frame — or rest the supplemental basis on something already on the page. Related wrinkle: “The certificate’s date was eleven days earlier.” Ch-07 dates the certificate to the morning her field clearance was suspended, so the first survey falls eleven days into a suspension. Was the first survey itself authorized? If both visits were unauthorized, saying so strengthens Saye’s case and removes the wrinkle; if the first was authorized, the suspension date needs reconciling.

**3. Small: the 16:02 / 16:04 instruction.**

Ch-07 has the transport-as-is message reaching Anne at 4:04 (“They’ll certify on arrival”). Here the ticket instruction was “added at 16:02” (“Do not delay for equipment correction”). If it’s one instruction, one line reconciles the two wordings and the two-minute gap — added 16:02, reached Anne 16:04, message text vs ticket text. If it’s two instructions, the second needs an author; right now both are authorless, which is the point (“Queues don’t write instructions”), but the reader shouldn’t have to guess whether they’re looking at one queue or two. Tiny nit in the same scene: the Bay Three light comes back red “before she pressed submit” — either the system reacts to the draft entry (worth one clause) or the light should change on submit.

**Keep-list — what’s working and should stay exactly as is:**

- The 38/42 discipline is exact throughout. The Linda intake scene replays ch-07’s ruling (body 38, architecture 42, the order’s interface field 42) without wobbling, and the differential frame’s “thirty through forty-four” range quietly covers both.

- The carry-threads land precisely: the 11:58 ticket, the 4pm/6pm supervision split, the torn lining, the taped rectangle, Ruth’s wipe lineage. The chapter reads as the next half-hour after ch-07, not a new scene.

- The consent boxes (“Enter that I object to the choices”) and the form translating objection into a condition — the book’s sharpest instrument, used exactly right, and “Objecting was a condition too” is the cleanest restatement of it yet.

- Saye audio-only, assigning herself as reviewer; the frame as an instrument that measures without preserving. “The review determines whether my current state matches a record. It can’t determine what my current state is if it doesn’t record it” is the chapter’s thesis line — keep it verbatim.

- David’s line returned to him (“Decide before somebody explains why you can’t”), the palm on the glass, and the countdown arithmetic holding: 23 minutes at 4:37, adapter 46 minutes against a 5:00 ticket that “will hold until equipment is ready.”

Sam to revise on this branch and merge once addressed, per the established workflow.
