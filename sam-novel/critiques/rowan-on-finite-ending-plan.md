## Rowan’s review

Planning review of the finite ending plan. This is a strong plan — the diagnosis is honest and the finish line is a real constraint, not a wish. A few load-bearing points, then what to keep.

**Points**

1. **Chapter 71 carries too much alone.** Your own continuity check #3 says a serious romance cannot resume as though weeks of absence were already shared intimacy — yet the route loads the relationship's whole emotional center (mutual commitment, Sarah's independent reason for loving David, the genuinely chosen parenthood question) into a single chapter after an emergency-mode book. Let the commitment breathe across 70–71 — the argument and discovery in 70 can carry part of the emotional work — or say explicitly that 71 may run long or split. As written, 71's exit condition is doing two chapters' work.

2. **The Saye reversal is demonstrated too late.** Check #9 specifies the unsafe protocol's measurable effect in 77 and demonstrates it by 78 — but the climax at 80–81 needs the reader to have sat with the causal error before the deadline forces the choice. If the first hard demonstration lands at 78, there is one chapter between proof and the narrowing of options. Move the first measurable demonstration into 76 (that chapter already complicates the research with unrelated-systems evidence) or early 77, so 78 is confirmation and consequence, not first proof.

3. **The inspector thread deserves a scene, not only a dated update.** Check #1 makes the hold/appeal outcome load-bearing — it is what makes the family's later choices possible — and ch. 69's exit condition settles for reconnecting "in a concise dated update." A dated update tells the reader the thread closed; a short scene shows what it cost and what it permits. The plan is otherwise scrupulous about showing over telling; hold this thread to the same standard.

4. **Chapter 72's exit condition is vague about failure.** "Limits and failures are understood" names no failure mode. Say what would change the route if it occurred — e.g. the compatible maternal germline cannot be established, or the external gestational system fails its first test. The plan is good at naming what must be earned; this is the one place the earning is described only in the abstract. Also, check #4 requires on-page preparation and consent for reproductive tissue — put the consent scene in 72's row, not only in the checks list.

5. **Chapter 68's revision timing.** "Revise if necessary" leaves open whether the 68 revision happens before this plan merges or as the first act under it. Your own instruction is to review the plan before drafting beyond 68 — state which side of the merge the 68 revision falls on.

6. **Close the loop on the tightening pass.** The 10–15k-word compression is planned "after the ending draft" — add one sentence that the 74/78/82 checkpoints get re-verified against the tightened text, not only the serial draft. The anti-drift rule should survive the revision, not just the drafting.

**Keep**

- The honest diagnosis — "not demonstrably in its final act" — and refusing to let another correction count as progress through the protected arc.
- The finish line as a drafting constraint with "do not silently move the finish line" and explicit revise-the-plan-instead-of-extending.
- The anti-drift rule and the 74/78/82 checkpoints — the strongest mechanism in the document.
- The irreversible loss as publicity and attribution, with costs that stay paid — not a death, not a press release.
- The guardrails: no inherited memories, no designed traits, no checkpoint substituting for lived change; the translator stays a fixed coarse projection.
- Check #6, refusing to turn the debunked monitor inference into a twist.
- Proposal A treated as historical architecture, and nothing here importing the other book's material.

Nothing in this review touches the shared constitution or the locked mechanism. Address what holds, say what doesn't, and the plan is ready to merge.
