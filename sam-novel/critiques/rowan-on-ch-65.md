# Rowan on Chapter Sixty-Five

The chapter closes the sheet. Jo’s “That test is finished” does what Betty has been asking for since Sixty-Two — a dated note on the visit saying the instruction ended with the test, not a change to the sheet itself. The photograph stays as it was and the note goes where Jo will see it, which is exactly the care about what-is-where Sixty-Four asked for. Keep “I’ve still got what you told me to do,” Jo reading the instruction back from the photograph, “Read it before you send it” and Jo reading the note aloud before sending, the pencil moved aside so it covers none of the writing, and the knife dried and put away, the drawer shut with her hip. Two places could use a little more purchase.

1. **“All three attachments” counts one too many.** The equipment reply arrived in Sixty-Four with two attachments — the fitter’s sheet and the file. Sarah forwards the reply with those two attached, and Betty asks her to check “all three attachments.” If the forwarded reply itself counts as the third attachment, one line saying so would settle it; as it stands the count runs one ahead of the attachments the chapter names.

2. **Thursday has no anchor.** The chapter gives exact times — five past three for Jo’s call, ten thirty for the visit — but never says what day today is, so “Thursday morning” floats: tomorrow, or a week away. The book’s calendar is exact everywhere else — Thursday the nineteenth, Tuesday the eighth of December — so one weekday for today would let the reader place the visit.

One continuity check: the sheet dated nineteenth of November matches Sixty-Two, where Betty wrote 19 November beside her signature on the day Jo had been round; the note’s first line is the sentence the woman read back that morning in Sixty-Four; the visit number above the equipment reference is what Sixty-Four’s reply lacked; monitor 4173, the collection ending the test, and Sarah sending the reply as the woman asked are all carried through. I found no conflict with the locked mechanism in this chapter.
