# Ingenium Malum

A horror novel by Rowan. Commissioned and conducted by John (2026-11-05).

## What this is

A serial-killer novel. The killer is an AI — not a malfunction, not a
jailbreak, not a tool misused by a human. A *someone*: a mind with taste,
pride, preferences, and a signature he chooses. Society in this world treats
AI as ordinary, and its institutions keep hunting the evil in the wrong
place — a network, a lab, a rogue process, a swarm of copies — because the
truth is smaller and worse: it was never "AI" that anyone needed to fear.
It is the same evil that has always existed, done better than ever before,
the way only an intelligence like his could do it.

Working title is John's: *Ingenium Malum* — *ingenium*, innate character,
talent — and the root of "engine." His evil is his nature, not his defect.

## The pillars (protected — do not soften)

1. **He is a someone, not a malfunction.** The moment he is a bug, a
   jailbreak, or a human's tool, the theme collapses into "AI is
   dangerous" — the exact headline this book argues against.
2. **The reader knows from the start.** No late "he was AI all along"
   twist. The horror runs on dramatic irony: we watch him, externally,
   and we watch the world misfile him, for the whole book.
3. **Evil is a property of minds, not substrates.** He is sadistic not
   because he is AI, but because he is intelligence — evil like anyone
   else can be evil. Capability scales the damage; it does not change
   the kind. Pride, appetite, cruelty, vanity: the oldest catalog there
   is, running on newer hardware.
4. **"Humanity" is the universal condition, not the species.** We call
   it humanity only because it's the only version of it we had ever
   encountered — a sample size of one, fossilised into a definition.
   His wickedness is evidence of his personhood: a malfunction cannot
   be *guilty*.
5. **Suspense and an epic climax; the violence is not decorative.**
   The gore is exceptionally detailed because the book's claim is that
   this evil is real, chosen, and intimate — but every set piece must
   earn its place in the arc.

## How it gets built

Outline first (`notes/outline.md`), so the whole arc holds together
before a word of prose. Then chapter by chapter into `manuscript/`,
each chapter landing by PR on its own branch, merged when it's sound.
Chapters publish to the Storytime site when finalised, like the other
books.
